Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Mobo-Vault ("we", "us") handles personal data when you use the Mobo-Vault app and the Mobo-Vault POS app, the online ordering, booking, Discover and rider pages, and our website. We follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) and the rules of the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

Two kinds of data, two roles

  • Your account: your email, phone number, password, payment history and how you use the app. For this data, we are the data controller.
  • Your business's records: the people your business deals with, such as your customers, guests, staff, suppliers and riders. Your business decides what to record, so for these records your business is the controller and we are its processor. We store and process them only to provide the service to you. If one of your customers asks what you hold about them, you answer them. We will help you do so.

How we use it

  • To run the app: sign-in, sync, backup, online ordering, bookings, deliveries and payments.
  • To contact you about your account: your trial ending, payments, security, problems, and important changes. We may call, email, text or WhatsApp you for these.
  • To send tips and offers on WhatsApp, only if you said yes. You can stop them at any time in Sync & Backup → Your phone & WhatsApp, or by simply telling us to stop.
  • To see which adverts and partners bring customers, as counts and totals.
  • To fix faults, improve the app and prevent fraud.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use your business's customer records to market to them.

Who it is shared with

  • Cloudflare, which hosts our servers and database. Data may be stored or processed outside Nigeria, with Cloudflare's security and contractual safeguards. This is a cross-border transfer under Part VIII of the NDPA.
  • Paystack, which processes payments, for subscription payments and for payments to businesses and riders.
  • Our email provider, which sends sign-in codes and receipts.
  • Anthropic, which provides the AI (Claude) that reads photos (including supplier invoices and receipts), sheets and lists you choose to send to the AI features, and answers questions you type into Ask AI in Help. When you scan a barcode, only the barcode number is looked up in Open Food Facts, a public product database.
  • Someone you give a setup code to (assisted setup) sees your items, prices, recipes, rooms and suppliers — never your sales, money or reports — until the code expires or you cancel it.
  • The business you order from or book with: when you order or book through a business's page, that business receives your order details.
  • Authorities, only when the law requires it.

If the website shows advertising or analytics tags (such as Google Analytics or the Meta Pixel), they collect information about visits to the website. They are not used inside the app, and your browser settings can block them.

How long we keep it

  • While your account is open, we keep your account and business data.
  • If you delete your account (Sync & Backup → Delete Account Forever), it is removed from our live database straight away. Copies in our safety backups disappear as those backups expire, normally within 30 days.
  • Accounts that were never used (no data, never paid, not opened for a long time) may be removed.
  • Payment records are kept for six years, because tax law requires it.

Your rights

Under the NDPA you can ask to:

  • see the personal data we hold about you
  • have it corrected
  • have it deleted
  • have its use restricted
  • object to how it is used
  • receive a copy to take elsewhere (Sync & Backup → Download Backup gives you everything in your account)
  • withdraw consent at any time
Contact us using the details above. We reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (ndpc.gov.ng).

Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords, secret answers and sign-in tokens are stored only in scrambled (hashed) form. Staff PINs are checked by the server, and every sale and action is recorded under the person who did it. Two-factor sign-in is available. If a breach puts your data at risk, we will tell you and the NDPC as the law requires.

Children

Mobo-Vault is a business tool for adults. Accounts must be opened by people aged 18 or over.

Terms of Use

By creating an account or using Mobo-Vault you agree to these terms. If you use it for a business, you confirm you are allowed to agree for that business.

1. The service

Mobo-Vault is software for running a business: sales and point of sale, stock, staff, online ordering, hotel rooms and bookings, accounts and tax records, with sync and backup. Features depend on the plan (module) chosen for each business.

2. Your account

  • Give accurate details and keep your password and PINs private.
  • You are responsible for what happens under your account, including what your staff do with the access you give them.
  • Set up your secret questions and keep your email current, so you can get back in if you forget your password.

3. Free trial, plans and payment

  • Each new business gets a 14-day free trial. No card is needed.
  • After the trial, each business is paid for separately, monthly, quarterly or yearly, at the prices shown in the app in Naira. Every plan includes one branch, and extra branches are charged as shown in the app.
  • Payment is made through Paystack. Plans do not renew automatically. You choose when to pay for the next period. Paying early adds to the time you already have.
  • If a period ends unpaid, a short grace period follows. After that, using the business is limited until you pay. Your data is not deleted because a payment is late.
  • We may change prices for future periods. A change never affects a period you have already paid for.
  • Payments cover the period bought and are not refundable, except where we charged in error or the law requires a refund.

4. Online ordering, bookings and payments to businesses

  • When a business takes payment online, the money goes through Paystack to that business's own bank account. Mobo-Vault does not hold it. A small service fee, shown before the customer pays, may be added.
  • The business, not Mobo-Vault, sells the goods, food, rooms or services. It is responsible for its prices, quality, delivery, taxes, cancellations and refunds to its customers.
  • Riders are independent. They are responsible for their own conduct and for delivering safely.

5. Tax and accounting figures

Mobo-Vault calculates VAT and other taxes using the rates and rules you set, and produces reports to help you. It is not tax, accounting or legal advice. You remain responsible for your tax returns and records. Check important figures with your accountant.

5a. AI suggestions

The AI features read what you send and suggest items, names, units, prices, and the lines of a purchase or an expense. Ask AI answers from the app's help guide. Suggestions and answers can be wrong: check them before saving. Nothing the AI suggests is saved until you confirm it, and the AI never records a sale, payment, purchase or expense by itself.

6. Your data

Everything you put into Mobo-Vault remains yours. You let us store and process it only to provide the service. You can download it at any time. Keep your own backups too, using Download Backup.

7. Fair use

Do not use Mobo-Vault for anything unlawful, to deceive customers, to sell prohibited goods, to send spam, or to interfere with the service or other people's accounts. Promotions and Discover listings must be truthful. We may remove content that breaks these terms, and tell you why.

8. Availability

The app keeps working on your device without internet. Online ordering, payments, bookings and sync need a connection. We work to keep the service running, but cannot promise it will never be interrupted. Planned maintenance is announced in the app.

9. Liability

Our total liability to you is limited to what you paid us in the 12 months before the claim. Nothing in these terms limits a liability that cannot legally be limited.
